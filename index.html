Method

How the numbers are produced.

Football model

For every team the system keeps its last 15 results (home and away separately). The expected goals of each side are derived from the weighted average of total goals in recent matches (most recent 5 matches weigh 1.5, next 5 weigh 1.0, older 0.7) and combined into a Poisson model that gives the probability of every scoreline from 0-0 to 5-5.

From those scorelines the site computes 1X2, Double Chance, Over/Under 1.5 and 2.5, Goal/No Goal. Confidence depends on how stable the teams' scoring has been (dispersion of results): high, medium or low. A team needs at least 8 recorded matches before any prediction is published.

A market is strong when its probability exceeds its own threshold (e.g. 1 or 2 ≥ 70%, Over 1.5 ≥ 72%, Under 2.5 ≥ 65%). Top Picks are the strong markets of high-confidence matches, ranked by probability (max 20 per day). Each pick is frozen before kick-off and graded on the 90-minute score; the Report and Archive show every result, wins and losses alike.

Tennis model

Prices from the sharpest bookmaker are converted into margin-free probabilities. Only favourites between 68% and 82% in ATP, WTA and Challenger main-draw singles with a low market margin are published, with fair odds (1/p) and the minimum odds worth taking.

Data & updates

Fixtures and scores are refreshed automatically every 30 minutes; tennis picks are recalculated every 30 minutes and graded automatically.
